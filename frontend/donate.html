Why Clothing Donation Matters

Extending a garment's life by just 9 months reduces its carbon, water, and waste footprints by around 20–30%.

Massive Water Conservation

Producing a single cotton t-shirt requires approximately 2,700 liters of fresh water. Reusing existing textiles preserves vital freshwater resources.

Curbing Microplastics & Carbon

Synthetic garments (polyester, nylon) take up to 200 years to decompose in landfills. Keeping them circulating prevents soil leaching and methane emissions.

Dignified Direct Support

Rather than shipping discarded clothes overseas to end up in foreign deserts, ReWear connects items directly with individuals, charities, and shelters.
