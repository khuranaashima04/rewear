Simple 4-Step Cycle

How ReWear Works

Keep valuable textiles circulating in use rather than deteriorating in landfills.

STEP 01

Upload Unused Clothes

Take photos of garments sitting unworn in your wardrobe and write a quick description.

STEP 02

Choose Listing Type

Decide whether to sell for cash, swap for another item, or donate to support individuals in need.

STEP 03

Connect & Confirm

Review buyer orders or swap proposals with clear notifications and secure approvals.

STEP 04

Give Clothes New Life

Ship or meet locally. Track your personal environmental impact on our live dashboard.

Why ReWear

A Fashion Platform That Puts the Planet First

Traditional fast fashion generates over 92 million tons of textile waste each year. ReWear changes that narrative by providing an integrated ecosystem where unwanted garments can be traded, sold, or freely shared.

Genuine Circular Economy

Direct clothing exchange lets you refresh your wardrobe with zero monetary cost.

Community Giving & Social Impact

Support families and students directly with warm coats, kids clothes, and daily essentials.

Verified & Safe Transactions
